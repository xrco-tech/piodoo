Chat2Work

Terms and privacy notice

Last updated 29 October 2026
In short
  • Chat2Work is free. We will never ask you for money or your bank details.
  • We keep your details so recruiters and employers can find you for work, and to help you with your CV.
  • Your SA ID number is stored encrypted and is never shown to recruiters or employers.
  • We keep your information for 12 months after you last use Chat2Work, or less if you ask us to delete it.
  • You can see, correct or delete your information at any time: email xolani@chat2work.co.za.

1. Who we are

Chat2Work is run by XR Co. (Pty) Ltd (Xolani Radebe Co.), Johannesburg, South Africa. We are the "responsible party" for your personal information under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Our Information Officer is Xolani Radebe, xolani@chat2work.co.za.

2. What Chat2Work does

You can join Chat2Work by dialling *134*433*48# or by sending Ispani on WhatsApp. Chat2Work:

  • adds you to a workforce database that recruiters and employers use to find people for work;
  • helps you upload or create a CV;
  • lets you ask a Chat2Work agent to call you back;
  • keeps you updated by WhatsApp or SMS.

Joining Chat2Work does not guarantee you a job or an interview. Chat2Work is for people aged 18 to 34 with a South African ID number.

3. What we collect

  • Your phone number, and whether you use it for WhatsApp.
  • Your SA ID number, to check your age and that you only register once. It is stored encrypted and is never shown on USSD or WhatsApp screens.
  • Your work situation: your employment status and whether you're looking for work.
  • Your CV, and anything you enter on your CV page: name, email address, town, education, work experience, skills, languages and driver's licence.
  • Your messages and choices: what you send us on WhatsApp, the options you choose on USSD, callback requests, and notes our agents make when they speak to you.
  • How you found us, if you tell us (for example a flyer or poster).

We don't collect anything until you choose I agree. If you don't agree, we don't keep your details.

4. Why we use it

  • To create and keep your Chat2Work profile.
  • To match you with work, and to show your profile and CV to recruiters and employers.
  • To create your CV, and to help you with it if you ask.
  • To contact you by WhatsApp, SMS or phone about your profile, your CV and work opportunities.
  • To keep the service safe, for example to stop duplicate registrations and fraud.
  • To understand how Chat2Work is used, using totals that don't identify you.

We use your information because you agreed to it. You can withdraw your consent at any time (see section 8).

5. Who sees your information

XR Co. and the Chat2Work teamTo run the service, support you and call you back.
Recruiters and employersYour profile and CV, so they can consider you for work. They don't see your ID number.
Service providersCompanies that carry our messages or help us run the service: Meta (WhatsApp), Africa's Talking (USSD), SMS providers, Cloudflare (website security), and AI tools that help our agents summarise conversations and draft replies. They may only use your information to provide their service to us.

Some of these providers process information outside South Africa. We only use providers that are bound by laws or agreements that protect your information to a standard similar to POPIA.

We never sell your information.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information for 12 months after you last used Chat2Work (for example, the last time you dialled, sent us a message or updated your CV). After that we delete it, or keep only totals that don't identify you. If you ask us to delete your information sooner, we will.

7. How we protect it

Your ID number is encrypted, and only the Chat2Work team can see your full profile. Our website and CV pages use secure (HTTPS) connections. Your CV link is personal: don't share it, as anyone with the link can open your CV page while it's valid.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we have about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct anything that's wrong;
  • delete your information, or remove you from Chat2Work;
  • stop contacting you about work opportunities.

You can also withdraw your consent at any time. This stops us using your information from then on.

To do any of this, email xolani@chat2work.co.za, or dial *134*433*48# and choose Request Callback. We'll reply within 30 days. We may ask you a few questions to confirm it's you.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa: inforegulator.org.za.

9. Costs

Chat2Work doesn't charge you anything. Your network may charge for USSD sessions, and normal data or SMS rates may apply when you use WhatsApp or open your CV page.

10. Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your information, we'll tell you by WhatsApp or SMS.

Chat2Work will never ask you for money, airtime or your bank details. If someone does, it isn't us. Please report it to xolani@chat2work.co.za.
